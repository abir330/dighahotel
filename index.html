Explore Digha & Surroundings

Top 20 Places to Visit in & Around Digha

Hotel Dollhouse in Barrister Colony is perfectly positioned for exploring all of Digha's coastal landmarks, majestic temples, and scenic natural wonders.

01

Old Digha Sea Beach

ওল্ড দীঘা সমুদ্রসৈকত

Historic sea wall promenade famous for dramatic crashing waves, sea bathing ghat, and vibrant souvenir shopping.

📍 250m · 2 min walk from Dollhouse
02

New Digha Sea Beach

নিউ দীঘা সমুদ্রসৈকত

Expansive sandy shoreline lined with casuarina pine groves, perfect for gentle swimming, camel rides, and sunbathing.

📍 2.2 km · 6 min ride
03

Shree Jagannath Temple

শ্রী জগন্নাথ মন্দির, দীঘা

Architectural masterpiece mirroring the iconic Puri Jagannath temple, a grand spiritual beacon for travelers.

📍 1.8 km · 5 min ride
04

Amarabati Park & Ropeway

অমরাবতী পার্ক

Lush landscaped lakeside park offering paddle boating, tranquil walks, and an aerial ropeway ride with panoramic views.

📍 2.0 km · 6 min ride
05

Digha Science Centre

দীঘা বিজ্ঞান কেন্দ্র

Interactive science park, digital planetarium, and 3D cinema that delights kids and curious travelers alike.

📍 1.6 km · 4 min ride
06

Marine Aquarium (MARC)

মেরিন অ্যাকোয়ারিয়াম

Zoological Survey of India facility showcasing exotic deep-sea marine life, coral species, rays, and local sea fauna.

📍 1.2 km · 3 min ride
07

Digha Mohona Estuary

দীঘা মোহনা

Scenic river confluence and lively wholesale fish auction harbor where deep-sea fishing trawlers unload daily catches.

📍 3.5 km · 8 min ride
08

Dheu Sagar Park

ঢেউসাগর পার্ক

Modern seaside recreational park with musical fountain, open promenade benches, and twilight ocean views.

📍 1.0 km · 3 min ride
09

Biswa Bangla Park

বিশ্ব বাংলা পার্ক

Beautified garden space featuring iconic Biswa Bangla globe sculptures, floral displays, and evening photo points.

📍 1.4 km · 4 min ride
10

Digha Welcome Gate

দীঘা গেট

Grand architectural gateway spanning the national highway, warmly greeting travelers entering the coastal resort city.

📍 4.5 km · 10 min drive
11

Udaipur Sea Beach

উদয়পুর সমুদ্রসৈকত

Untouched virgin beach right on the Odisha border with serene Casuarina forest and quieter waves for relaxed sun-soaking.

📍 4.8 km · 12 min ride
12

Shankarpur Beach & Harbour

শংকরপুর সমুদ্রসৈকত

Peaceful twin beach known for pristine yellow sands, sea-gravel coastlines, and a working fishing boat jetty.

📍 14 km · 25 min drive
13

Tajpur Sea Beach

তাজপুর সমুদ্রসৈকত

Famous for millions of scurrying red ghost crabs across the sand and calm, uncrowded beachfront pine trails.

📍 18 km · 30 min drive
14

Mandarmani Beach

মন্দারমণি সমুদ্রসৈকত

One of India's longest drive-in beach coastlines with water sports, banana boat rides, and seaside shacks.

📍 28 km · 45 min drive
15

Talsari Sea Beach

তালসারী সমুদ্রসৈকত

Picturesque palm-fringed coast across the Subarnarekha river border where fishing skiffs rest in tidal creeks.

📍 8.5 km · 18 min drive
16

Chandaneswar Shiva Temple

চন্দনেশ্বর শিব মন্দির

Centuries-old revered pilgrimage temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, famous for its ancient architecture and annual Chadak mela.

📍 7.0 km · 15 min drive
17

Baba Bhusandheswar Temple

বাবা ভুসঙ্গেশ্বর মন্দির

Home to one of the largest black granite Shiva Lingams in all of Asia, located in a tranquil rural village setting.

📍 22 km · 40 min drive
18

Bichitrapur Mangrove Sanctuary

বিচিত্রপুর ম্যানগ্রোভ

Eco-tourism coastal mangrove forest offering boat safaris through delta channels and sighting migratory sea birds.

📍 26 km · 45 min drive
19

Dariapur Lighthouse

দারিয়াপুর বাতিঘর

Historic coastal lighthouse guiding maritime ships into the Bay of Bengal; climb up for bird's eye coastal views.

📍 40 km · Day trip
20

Kapalkundala Temple

কপালকুণ্ডলা মন্দির

Heritage shrine immortalized in Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay's famous literary masterpiece Kapalkundala.

📍 42 km · Day trip

Stay Guidelines & Transparency

Hotel Policies & Stay Guidelines

Clear, honest policies to ensure every guest enjoys a comfortable, secure, and relaxing stay in Old Digha.

All adult guests must present an original government-issued photo identity proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Indian Passport, or Driving License) during check-in as mandated by West Bengal Police and District Administration regulations. PAN cards and corporate ID cards are not accepted as valid address proofs.
Hotel Dollhouse warmly welcomes families, married couples, and responsible adult couples (18 years and above with valid original government ID cards). We take pride in preserving a peaceful, safe, and respectful atmosphere within the quiet residential enclave of Barrister Colony.
Guests who book directly through our WhatsApp channel receive flexible terms: free cancellation or date changes are accepted up to 48 hours prior to your scheduled check-in time. During peak holiday seasons (such as Durga Puja, Kali Puja/Diwali, and New Year week), specific cancellation terms will be stated in your WhatsApp confirmation.
We accept all convenient Indian payment methods including Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM UPI, IMPS/NEFT bank transfers, and cash at front desk check-in. A modest advance deposit confirms your reservation, with the remainder payable upon room check-in.
Children below 6 years of age stay complimentary when sharing existing bedding with parents. For additional adult guests or children above 6 years, an extra mattress with fresh bedsheets, pillow, and blanket can be provided for a nominal charge (from ₹[EXTRA]/night). Please specify the total guest count when checking availability on WhatsApp.
